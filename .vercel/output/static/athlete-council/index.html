Purest Athlete Council

More than an ambassador.
Part of the brand.

The Purest Athlete Council is our inner circle — a small group of athletes and active people who live this lifestyle for real. You help shape what we make next, and you're the face of why it matters.

What it means to be on the Council

This isn't a transactional program. We're not looking for people to post and disappear. Council members are people Pam handpicks because they genuinely represent what Purest stands for — clean ingredients, real performance, and a life spent outdoors and in motion.

You'll get to know Pam personally. You'll be asked for real feedback. And your community will see you as someone who helped build something — not just promote it.

Who we're looking for

Runners. Skiers. Surfers. Hikers. CrossFitters. Cyclists. Mountain athletes. Beach athletes. Anyone who pushes their body and cares deeply about what goes into it.

We don't require a certain follower count. We care about your values, your voice, and your community's trust in you. Some of our best council members have 2,000 followers and more influence than accounts with 200,000.

"I started Purest because I couldn't find anything clean enough for my own kids. The athletes I trust most are the ones who actually read the label. That's who I want on this council."

— Pam Sweeney, Founder
