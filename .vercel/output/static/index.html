I made it for myself first. Then everything changed.

I ended up in the hospital with hyponatremia. For years I couldn't understand what had really happened to me. Or why I kept feeling depleted no matter what I did. I tried every electrolyte on the market. Nothing worked. Nothing was clean. Nothing was real.

We live at 9,000 feet in Aspen, Colorado. I have four kids, all high-level athletes. The altitude, the exertion, the sweat — we needed something that actually worked. So I stopped looking for it and started making it. Real freeze-dried fruit. French gray sea salt. Nothing else.

Once I understood what an electrolyte actually needed to have in it, my life changed. And so did the lives of my children.
